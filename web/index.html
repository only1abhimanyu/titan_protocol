A tutor that finds the broken idea.

Three things every session does that a quiz app cannot.

Names the wrong idea

Every distractor is tagged with the misconception it reveals. Two of them agree and it tells you the sentence in your head that is wrong.

Traces the gap upstream

A failure pushes belief down the prerequisite chain, so a gap in the call stack shows up when you miss a question about recursion.

Makes you explain it

A correct answer you cannot explain does not move your mastery. You write it in your own words and it is graded against a rubric.
